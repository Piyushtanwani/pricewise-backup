# Functional Requirements: PriceWise

## 1. User and Account Management
- **FR-001 - FR-004:** User Registration, Authentication, Logout, and User Profile Management.
- **FR-005:** Authentication Error Handling.

## 2. Product Discovery
- **FR-006 - FR-010:** Product Search (keywords, brands), Search Suggestions, Search Result Ranking, Product Filtering (price, brand, rating), and Product Sorting.

## 3. Product Information
- **FR-011 - FR-014:** Display Product Details (name, specs, price), Specification Display, Product Source Attribution, and Product Availability.

## 4. Price Comparison
- **FR-015 - FR-019:** Multi-Source Price Comparison, Lowest Available Price Identification, Price Difference Analysis, Price Timestamp, and Price Data Refresh.
- **FR-020 - FR-023:** Product Selection for Comparison, Side-by-Side Comparison, Specification Difference Identification, and Comparison Price Analysis.

## 5. Historical Price Intelligence
- **FR-024 - FR-028:** Historical Price Storage, Retrieval, Visualization (graphs), Historical Price Statistics, and Price Trend Identification.

## 6. AI/ML Price Prediction & Recommendations
- **FR-029 - FR-034:** Prediction Data Preparation, Price Prediction, Prediction Horizon, Confidence Indicator, Data Sufficiency check, and Prediction Disclaimer.
- **FR-035 - FR-039:** Recommendation Generation, Buy/Wait/Monitor Recommendations, and Recommendation Explanation.

## 7. Watchlist, Monitoring, and Alerts
- **FR-040 - FR-043:** Add/Remove Product to Watchlist, View Watchlist, Watchlist Price Monitoring.
- **FR-044 - FR-046:** Configure Target Price, Trigger Price Alert, Manage Price Alerts.

## 8. Dashboard and Administration
- **FR-047 - FR-048:** Personalized Dashboard (tracked products, recent changes), Recent Activity.
- **FR-049 - FR-052:** Product Data Management, User Management, Data Quality Monitoring, System Monitoring.
